Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or password?

No password needed. Nexus uses the iCloud account signed into your device to identify you. Just make sure you're signed into iCloud in the Settings app, then create your profile in Nexus.

The app says it can't connect or my posts aren't syncing.

Check that you're signed into iCloud (Settings → your name) and that you have an internet connection. If both look fine, force-quit the app and reopen it — it syncs with iCloud on every launch. Still stuck? Email us with a description of what you're seeing.

How do I edit my profile?

Open the Portfolio tab and tap edit. You can update your name, photo, bio, major, graduation year, roles, and links at any time. To change your base profile or its details, tap Edit on the styled section of your Portfolio, or on the Now page in Discover.

What is a base profile (Filmmaker, Actor, or Musician)?

When you create an account you choose one base profile. It shapes three things:

  • Recommendations. The Home feed leads with the kind of work your profile is about — films and crew reels for filmmakers, performances for actors, music for musicians.
  • The Now page. The first page in Discover shows what you're working on and what you could get on next (see below).
  • Your profile layout. Actors get a casting-style page: headshot, reel, stats such as playable age range and height, and a resume of every role. Filmmakers get a filmography grouped by job, newest first, plus a grid of stills. Musicians get a discography: songs, albums and EPs, and upcoming shows.

Your role tags (Director, Composer, Dancer, and so on) are separate. You can have many tags, but one base profile. You can switch it any time.

What does the Now page show?

It depends on your base profile. Actors see the projects they're cast in, the auditions they've logged, and open roles that fit their age range, gender, look, and skills, with an Audition button. Filmmakers see their projects in production and crews looking for the jobs their role tags cover. Musicians see their upcoming shows, composer openings on projects, and gigs, open mics, and jam sessions. An upcoming concert or show you're playing also appears on your profile.

How do I get on the Actors, Filmmakers, or Musicians page?

Once you've chosen a base profile and filled in its details, a moderator reviews it. Approved profiles appear on that page, which everyone can browse from the Now page and from the Directory in Network. If your profile is declined, editing it sends it back for another look. Switching to a different base profile starts a new review.

How do I report inappropriate content or another user?

Every post has a report option in its menu. Reports are reviewed and content that violates community standards is removed. For urgent concerns, email us directly.

How do I delete a post I shared?

Open the post and use its menu to delete it. It will be removed for everyone.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your profile and content from within the app, or email us at aidenweinstein11@gmail.com and we'll remove everything associated with your account.

Who can use Nexus?

Nexus is built for the UT Austin creative community — students, alumni, and collaborators. It's designed for users 13 and older.
